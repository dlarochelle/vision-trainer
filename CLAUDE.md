# CLAUDE.md

See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for the conventions governing this
repository. They apply in full to Claude Code sessions.

Points that most often need restating mid-session:

- The deliverable is **one self-contained `index.html`** — no build
  step, no bundler, no network calls, no external assets.
- Pitch framing: a tool to see better and read faster, stated as
  **goals under testing** with a "more testing needed" line. Never as
  results. Banned words and the no-named-condition rule in AGENTS.md
  still apply in full.
- The footer disclaimer in AGENTS.md is **verbatim and always
  visible**, and is not traded against scope or deadline.
- The two modes keep **two independent scores**. Never merge them.
- Where `README.md` or `docs/EVIDENCE.md` disagree with the build, the
  constants at the top of `index.html` are authoritative. See AGENTS.md.
